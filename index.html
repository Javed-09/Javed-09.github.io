About Us

Building Trust,
Delivering Quality

Desert Edge Construction & Maintenance LLC is a UAE-based company specializing in comprehensive construction and general maintenance services. We serve residential villas, commercial offices, and industrial properties across the Emirates.

Our team of skilled technicians and engineers brings professional expertise to every project — from routine maintenance and repairs to full-scale renovations and civil works. We are committed to completing every job on time, within budget, and to the highest standards of quality and safety.

Our Services

Comprehensive Construction & Maintenance Solutions

From daily maintenance to full renovations, we provide a complete range of services tailored to homes, offices, and commercial spaces across the UAE.

General Building Maintenance

Complete building maintenance solutions including structural repairs, fixture maintenance, and preventive maintenance programs for all property types.

Plumbing

Professional plumbing services including installations, leak repairs, pipe fitting, drainage solutions, and water system maintenance for residential and commercial properties.

Electrical Works

Safe and compliant electrical installations, wiring, panel upgrades, lighting solutions, and troubleshooting by qualified electricians.

AC Installation & Maintenance

Split and central AC installation, servicing, duct cleaning, gas top-up, and annual maintenance contracts to keep you cool year-round.

Painting

Interior and exterior painting with premium materials, decorative finishes, wallpaper installation, and touch-up work for villas and commercial spaces.

Carpentry

Custom woodwork, cabinet installation, door fitting, shelving, furniture repair, and wooden flooring solutions by skilled carpenters.

Tiling & Flooring

Ceramic, porcelain, and marble tile installation, grouting, flooring solutions, and surface preparation for bathrooms, kitchens, and living areas.

Gypsum & False Ceiling

Gypsum board partition walls, false ceiling installation, decorative ceilings, soundproofing, and POP work for modern interiors.

Masonry & Civil Works

Block work, plastering, concrete repairs, boundary walls, structural modifications, and general civil construction works.

Waterproofing

Roof, bathroom, balcony, and foundation waterproofing using industry-grade materials to protect your property from water damage and seepage.

Villa & Office Maintenance

Dedicated annual maintenance packages for villas and offices, covering AC, plumbing, electrical, painting, and general upkeep with priority response.

Renovation & Remodeling

Complete villa and office renovations, kitchen and bathroom makeovers, layout modifications, and turnkey remodeling projects delivered end to end.
